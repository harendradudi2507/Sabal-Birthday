A private note · 08 October

Dear Manisha,

Twenty-three. Somehow that sounds like a lot and absolutely nothing at the same time.

I could have simply sent you a birthday message. But somehow that didn't feel enough. So instead, I wanted to make you a little place on the internet where all these photographs could live together.

Looking through these pictures reminds me how many ordinary moments become special simply because of the people in them.

Thank you for all the laughter, the conversations, the random adventures, the annoying moments, and all the memories that somehow became important.

I hope your twenty-third year brings you happiness that stays, people who genuinely care about you, places you have always wanted to see, and countless moments that deserve another photograph.

Keep smiling. Keep being wonderfully yourself. And please keep making memories.

Happy 24th Birthday ♡
— Harendra
